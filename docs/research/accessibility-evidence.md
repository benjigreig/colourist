# Evidence: colour accessibility is left late and fails often

> Status: in progress. Started 28 October 2026 for the problem statement; to be completed for launch messaging. Plane: CLR-59.

## Question

What evidence backs the claim that colour accessibility is usually left until last, and fails often? Benji asked for more than two sources, for the problem statement now and for launch.

## Sources so far

| Source | Finding | Date |
|---|---|---|
| [WebAIM Million](https://webaim.org/projects/million/) | Low-contrast text on 83.9% of the top million home pages, averaging 34 instances per page. The most common issue detected. | 2026 |
| [HTTP Archive Web Almanac](https://almanac.httparchive.org/en/2025/accessibility) | About 30% of sites meet minimum text contrast, up from 22% in 2019. | 2025 |
| [GDS public sector monitoring](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/2024/12/17/what-gds-has-found-from-public-sector-accessibility-monitoring) | Colour contrast is first of four main issues across 1,203 UK public sector websites and 21 apps, January 2022 to September 2024. | 2024 |
| [Deliveroo Design](https://medium.com/deliveroo-design/brightening-up-accessibility-with-a-new-colour-system-5921915641ed) | After their rebrand, "only a few colours met level AA accessibility standards". | — |
| [WHO World report on vision](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241516570) | At least 2.2 billion people have a vision impairment. | 2019 |
| [Colour Blind Awareness](https://www.colourblindawareness.org/colour-blindness/) | Colour vision deficiency affects about 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women, around 300 million people. | — |

The design team accounts used for the jobs to be done (UX Collective, idealo and GoCardless) are listed in [jobs-to-be-done.md](../jobs-to-be-done.md#sources).
