# Colour theory reading list

Sources to draw on later, for research and for the explainers (US-11). Collected by Benji on 15 November 2026 from recommendations in a generative art community. Links and short descriptions only: no copies of the material (repo rule). Nothing here has been read for colouri.st yet. When a source is used, cite it where it's used and note it here.

| Source | What it covers | Why it may help |
|---|---|---|
| Bruce MacEvoy, [*The geometry of color perception*](https://www.handprint.com/HP/WCL/color2.html), handprint.com | How colour perception is modelled: colour spaces, hue, chroma and lightness. Very deep and dense | Background for hue drift (CLR-14) and the colour model (ADR 0001) |
| [*Modern colour theory for traditional and digital painting media*](http://www.huevaluechroma.com), huevaluechroma.com | The mechanics of colour vision, and how they bear on contrast, colour difference and mixing | Explainers on contrast and distinctness (CLR-16, CLR-17) |
| Maryville University, [*The Art of Color: Color Wheel & Color Relationships*](https://online.maryville.edu/liberal-arts-degrees/the-art-of-color) | A summary of colour relationships and ways of building palettes through history | Explainers for people without a design background (CLR-65) |
| James Gurney, *Color and Light: A Guide for the Realist Painter* (book), and his [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCTIoOLzT1jbxAcPW99xn0zQ) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/jamesgurneyart) | Colour and light for painters: short, with lots of pictures and diagrams | Plain-language explainers (US-11) |
| James Gurney, [*Color Wheel Part 1*](http://gurneyjourney.blogspot.com/2010/02/color-wheel-part-1.html) and [*Gamut Masking*](http://gurneyjourney.blogspot.com/2011/09/part-1-gamut-masking-method.html), Gurney Journey | The colour wheel, and limiting a palette to a shape on the wheel | Palette relationships, and explaining gamut limits (D-047) |
| Johannes Itten, *Kunst der Farbe* (*The Art of Color*) (book) | A foundational art-school text on colour contrast and harmony. Mostly text | Harmony, one of D-052's relationships |
