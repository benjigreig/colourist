Vividness Tuner

Pick the recovery and ceiling settings that look right, by eye. Each strip is one brand colour on a recipe's ladder, lightest to darkest. Lightness never changes, so no contrast figure moves.

You can also try a maximum gap between steps. Where two steps are further apart than that, steps are added evenly between them, marked with a bar above the swatch and a bold grade.

Recovery lifts each step's chroma from the source's own towards the most the screen can show at that step. Ceiling caps it at a share of that maximum. Each step takes the lower of the two. The bar below a swatch marks the step nearest the source colour.

0 holds the source's chroma. 1 is as vivid as the screen allows.
1 is no cap. Lower values tame loud steps.
